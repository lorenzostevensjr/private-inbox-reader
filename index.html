Private Inbox Reader

A personal, single-owner tool that lets its owner's AI assistant read their own Gmail and Google Calendar, without being able to change anything.

What it is

Private Inbox Reader is a Google sign-in client used by one person to connect their own Google accounts to an AI coding and productivity assistant running on their own computer. It is not a public service, and it isn't offered, sold, or advertised to anyone else.

What it can do

  • Read Gmail messages (Google permission: gmail.readonly).
  • Read Google Calendar events (Google permission: calendar.readonly).

What it cannot do

Because it only holds read-only permissions, Google blocks it from sending, forwarding, deleting, moving, or labelling email, and from creating or changing calendar events. It does not access Google Drive, Contacts, or any other Google service.
